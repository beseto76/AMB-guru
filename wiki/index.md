## Concepts
- [Active Metal Brazing (AMB)](concepts/ActiveMetalBrazing.md) — ceramic substrate bonding via Ag-Cu-Ti braze layer, used in power modules
- [Direct Bond Copper (DBC)](concepts/DirectBondCopper.md) — ceramic substrate bonding via oxidized copper, no active metal layer
- [Organic Direct-Bond Copper (ODBC)](concepts/OrganicDirectBondCopper.md) — experimental ceramic-free substrate on DuPont Temprion polyimide film
