## Summary
A Dataintelo market-research report sizing the global AMB-only substrate market at USD 372 million in 2025, growing to USD 968 million by 2034 at an 11.2% CAGR — the smallest 2025 base-year figure of any AMB-only estimate ingested so far. Segments by material (AlN 58.2%, Si3N4 32.1%, Al2O3 9.7%), application (Power Electronics 42.3%, Automotive 28.7%, Aerospace 15.2%, Industrial 13.8%), and end-user industry.

## Key Claims
- Global AMB-only market: USD 372M (2025) → USD 968M (2034), 11.2% CAGR (2026-2034).
- Asia Pacific dominates with 48.7% revenue share; Japan alone ~22.4% of global value.
- Material split: Aluminum Nitride 58.2% share (170-200 W/m·K, 12.4% CAGR — fastest-growing and largest), Silicon Nitride 32.1% (80-120 W/m·K, superior fracture toughness), Aluminum Oxide 9.7%.
- Top supplier ranking (2025 revenue share): Kyocera 18.2%, Rogers Corporation 14.7%, Heraeus Electronics 12.3%, NGK Electronics Devices 9.8%, Mitsubishi Materials 8.6%, DOWA Electronics Materials 7.2%; top 10 suppliers control ~62% of the market.
- Automotive OEM specs require AEC-Q200 certification, IATF 16949 systems, and 1000+ thermal cycles (-40°C to +125°C) reliability.
- Threats: Chinese competitors (Tong Hsing, KCC) intensifying price competition; Si3N4 ceramic powder supply concentration risk.
