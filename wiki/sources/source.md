## Summary
A Korean-language industry briefing from QYResearch Korea ("유망산업 브리핑" / Promising Industry Briefing) covering the global AMB (Active Metal Brazing) heat-dissipation substrate market, its competitive landscape (Top 3: Denka, Rogers, Ferrotec at 80% combined share), and three promising South Korean domestic companies: KCC첨단소재 (KCC Advanced Materials), 아모그린텍 (Amogreentech), and LX세미콘 (LX Semicon).

## Key Claims
- AMB substrate bonds copper circuitry to ceramic via an active metal interlayer (Ag-Cu-Ti paste), unlike DBC which bonds copper directly to ceramic; the active metal braze layer acts as a buffer that improves thermal reliability.
- AMB substrate types: SiN (Silicon Nitride) AMB and AlN (Aluminum Nitride) AMB; applications: automotive, traction & railway, new energy & power grid, military & aerospace, industrial and others.
- Global AMB substrate market: USD 376 million (2022e) → USD 2,563 million (2029f), CAGR 26.0% (2023–2029) — per QYResearch Korea.
- Competitive landscape: Top 3 players (Denka, Rogers, Ferrotec) hold 80% combined market share; remaining 20% ("Others") includes BYD, Shengda Tech, Toshiba Materials, Heraeus Electronics, Kyocera, NGK Electronics Devices, DOWA METALTECH, KCC, Amogreentech, Shenzhen Xinzhou Electronic, Zhejiang TC Ceramic Electronic, Wuxi Tianyang Electronics, and others.
- SiN AMB substrates are mainly used for EVs; higher mechanical durability and heat dissipation make AMB preferred for high-performance power modules subject to high voltage/temperature/vibration (EVs, rail, renewables).
- Promising South Korean companies: **KCC첨단소재 (KCC Advanced Materials)** — launched Si3N4 and high-strength AlN AMB substrates offering higher thermal conductivity/strength than conventional alumina substrates, targeting high-performance power semiconductor modules. **아모그린텍 (Amogreentech)** — developed long-life, high-heat-dissipation AlN AMB substrates; its core magnetic-materials products are used in Tesla and Rivian high-performance EVs, and it is growing heat-dissipation substrates as a new growth driver. **LX세미콘 (LX Semicon)** — building a new heat-dissipation substrate production plant, planned to start operation within 2023; uses diffusion bonding (no adhesive) to join ceramic and copper, achieving higher thermal/mechanical reliability.
