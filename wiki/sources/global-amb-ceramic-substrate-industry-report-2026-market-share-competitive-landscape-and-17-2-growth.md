## Summary
A QYResearch industry-report summary (published via note.com, August 2026) sizing the global AMB market at USD 639 million in 2025, growing to USD 2,033 million by 2032 at a 17.2% CAGR — an eighth/ninth distinct AMB-only market-size trajectory in this wiki, and the most recent (freshest) of the QYResearch-lineage figures. Also gives the most current company-level developments of any source ingested (April 2025-March 2026 window).

## Key Claims
- Global AMB market: USD 639M (2025) → USD 2,033M (2032), 17.2% CAGR (2026-2032).
- Top 10 companies held ~84.0% of 2025 revenue: Jiangsu Fulehua Semiconductor Technology, Rogers Corporation, BYD, NGK Corporation, Niterra Materials, Heraeus Electronics, Mitsubishi Materials, Zhejiang TC Ceramic Electronic, DOWA METALTECH, Proterial — notably ranks Jiangsu Fulehua Semiconductor Technology #1, ahead of Rogers, a ranking not seen in any other source.
- Company developments: (1) April 2025 — Ferrotec completed a new Si3N4-AMB/DCB power-substrate factory in Malaysia, entering full-scale production, focused initially on automotive inverters. (2) May 2025 — NGK Corporation exhibited SN-AMB/DCB substrates, SiC wafers, and high-current ceramic wiring substrates together at PCIM Europe 2025, positioning itself as a systems (not just component) supplier. (3) March 2026 — Heraeus Electronics announced an electrical life prediction model for Si3N4 ceramic substrates at CIPS 2026 (Germany), enabling quantitative insulation-reliability/service-life evaluation for power-module design and certification.
- Demand drivers: automotive main inverters, 800V platforms, renewable-energy converters; SiC+Si3N4 AMB combination meets high power density/thermal shock/long-term reliability needs. AlN AMB retains a niche for applications prioritizing raw thermal conductivity.
- Competitive axis has shifted from bonding technology alone to comprehensive capability: mass-production yield, automotive certification, raw-material procurement, and regional supply capacity.
- Lists "NGK Corporation" and "Niterra Materials" as two separate entries in its top-10 — likely reflecting NGK Insulators' 2023 global rebrand to Niterra for parts of its materials business, though the report does not clarify the distinction (open question).
