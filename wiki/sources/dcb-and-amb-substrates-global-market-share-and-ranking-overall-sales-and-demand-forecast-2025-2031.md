## Summary
A QYResearch report (distributed via GII Research, published 24 October 2025) covering the combined global DCB and AMB substrate market, valued at US$ 996M in 2024 and forecast to reach US$ 2,148M by 2031 at a 12.2% CAGR. Gives separate competitive rankings for DBC and AMB substrates, and repeats the same Japan/Germany/China production-share figures seen in the Intel Market Research source, suggesting a shared underlying data lineage.

## Key Claims
- Combined DCB + AMB substrate market: US$ 996M (2024) → US$ 2,148M (2031), CAGR 12.2% (2025–2031).
- DBC substrates: ceramic insulator (Al2O3 or AlN) with pure copper attached via high-temperature eutectic melting.
- AMB substrates: brazing pure copper onto AlN or Si3N4 ceramic in a high-temperature vacuum process (no standard metallization); enables double-sided copper weights up to 800μm on ceramics as thin as 0.25mm.
- DBC ceramic substrate market dominated by a few players: Rogers Corporation, Ferrotec, NGK Electronics Devices, KCC, Shengda Tech, BYD, Heraeus Electronics, and Nanjing Zhongjiang New Material — global top 5 players held >79.8% share in 2024. More Chinese entrants are appearing, driven by China's EV growth.
- AMB ceramic substrate key manufacturers: Rogers Corporation, Ferrotec, BYD, Toshiba Materials, Heraeus Electronics, Denka, Proterial, Mitsubishi Materials — global top 8 players held ~85% revenue share in 2023 (matches the figure in [[amb-ceramic-substrate-market-2026-to-2034-intel-market-research]] exactly).
- AMB production concentrated in Japan (27.3%), Germany (24.56%), China (43.5%) in 2023 — identical figures to those in [[amb-ceramic-substrate-market-2026-to-2034-intel-market-research]], strongly suggesting both reports draw on the same underlying QYResearch dataset. China's production share is expected to reach 57% by 2030.
- NGK Electronics Devices and Ferrotec have started/built AMB production factories in Malaysia; Southeast Asia is expected to become an important production base.

## Contradictions
- **Yet another combined-market total, inconsistent with the other combined-market source:** this report values the combined DCB+AMB market at US$ 996M in 2024, while [[dcb-and-amb-substrate-market-size-share-report-to-2035]] (Transparency Market Research) values the same combined market at US$ 2.1B in the same year, 2024 — roughly a 2.1x discrepancy for nominally the same market and base year. Combined with the earlier AMB-only discrepancies, this is now the fourth mutually-inconsistent market-sizing figure encountered across four market-research sources.
- Not a contradiction, but worth noting: this source's AMB production-share (Japan 27.3%/Germany 24.56%/China 43.5%) and top-8-player share (~85% in 2023) figures are identical to those in [[amb-ceramic-substrate-market-2026-to-2034-intel-market-research]], which suggests these two ostensibly independent "reports" may recycle the same primary QYResearch research rather than being fully independent corroborating sources — a reason for caution in treating agreement between them as strong confirmation.
