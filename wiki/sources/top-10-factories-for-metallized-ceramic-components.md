## Summary
A vendor blog post from Fuzhou Meetcera Technology Co., Ltd. (a Chinese metallized-ceramics manufacturer) ranking the "top 10" factories for metallized ceramic components (DBC, AMB, and HTCC technologies), covering specialization, capabilities, and notable customers/certifications for each. **Note: this is self-promotional vendor content** — Meetcera ranks itself #1 in its own listicle — so company-specific claims here should be treated as marketing framing rather than independent analysis, though the factual details about named competitors (customers supplied, product lines, certifications) are plausible and consistent with other sources.

## Key Claims
- Ranked list (rank, company, location, specialization/notable claim):
  1. **Meetcera** (China) — DBC & AMB ceramic substrates, metallized AlN; self-described as leading in DBC/AMB solutions with "advanced material expertise and strong R&D" (self-ranked #1, vendor bias caveat applies).
  2. **Kyocera Corporation** (Japan) — DBC/AMB substrates, HTCC packages; described as a "global power module leader supplying Tesla."
  3. **Heraeus Holding GmbH** (Germany) — high-precision Mo-Mn metallization, DBC/AMB substrates; described as a global leader in aerospace/medical vacuum sealing. (Parent of [[HeraeusElectronics]].)
  4. **Rogers Corporation** (USA) — curamik® AMB Si3N4 substrates with >90 W/m·K thermal conductivity; described as the "IGBT preferred choice for electric vehicles."
  5. **Jiangsu Fulehua Semiconductor** (China) — AMB/DBC ceramic-Cu substrates; described as having the largest domestic AlN metallization capacity in China, supplying BYD and Huawei.
  6. **Guoci Materials (Sinocera)** (China) — metallized AlN/Al2O3 powders and substrates; described as a pioneer in domestic (Chinese) electronic-grade ceramic materials with integrated powder+substrate manufacturing.
  7. **CoorsTek** (USA) — high-purity Al2O3/AlN metallized components, vacuum feedthroughs; described as a NASA supplier with military-grade reliability.
  8. **NGK/NTK** (Japan) — Si3N4 AMB substrates, metallized ceramics for sensors; described as a "core supplier for automotive electronics."
  9. **Zhejiang Jingci Semiconductor** (China) — DPC (Direct Plated Copper)/thin-film metallized ceramics; specializes in 5G filters and Mini LED backplanes (notably a different application space from power modules).
  10. **Maruwa Co., Ltd.** (Japan) — high-frequency HTCC metallized substrates, AlN DBC substrates; specializes in microwave communication and satellite applications.

## Key Quotes
> "Leading in DBC and AMB solutions for power modules and high-performance applications. Advanced material expertise and strong R&D." — Meetcera's self-description, ranked #1 in its own listicle.
