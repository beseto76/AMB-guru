## Summary
Rogers Corporation's own technical-expertise page describing its curamik® Active Metal Brazed (AMB) substrates — a second primary (vendor) source on Rogers, complementing [[curamik-metallized-ceramic-substrates]] with more detailed materials-science specifications.

## Key Claims
- curamik® AMB substrates consist of a Si3N4 (silicon nitride) ceramic isolator onto which pure copper is brazed in a high-temperature vacuum brazing process.
- AMB is the substrate of choice for automotive, wind turbines, traction applications, and high-voltage DC transmission — anywhere high reliability, heat dissipation, and low partial discharge are required.
- Si3N4 thermal conductivity: 90 W/m·K; supports copper layers up to 800 µm thick for high ampacity and heat spreading.
- Coefficient of thermal expansion comparison: Al2O3 7.1 ppm/K, Si3N4 2.6 ppm/K, AlN 4.7 ppm/K — all close to silicon's 4 ppm/K, which is why both DBC and AMB are suitable for robust bare-die packaging (low thermal-cycling wear-out risk).
- Rogers positions its DBC manufacturing experience as the technical base enabling its AMB substrate line (both Si3N4 and AlN), with existing structuring/inspection facilities and global sales/support.
