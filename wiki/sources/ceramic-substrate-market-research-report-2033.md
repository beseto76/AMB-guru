## Summary
A Dataintelo report sizing the *broader* ceramic substrate market (Alumina, AlN, BeO, Si3N4 — spanning automotive, consumer electronics, telecom, and industrial applications, not just power-module AMB/DBC) at USD 8.7 billion in 2025, growing to USD 14.2 billion by 2033 at a 6.8% CAGR. This is a different, larger market scope than the AMB-only or DCB+AMB power-module markets covered elsewhere in this wiki — useful as context for the materials landscape (Kyocera's overall scale) but not directly comparable to the AMB-specific figures.

## Key Claims
- Broader ceramic substrate market: USD 8.7B (2025) → USD 14.2B (2033), 6.8% CAGR.
- Product-type split: Alumina 42.5% (largest, $3.7B), Aluminum Nitride 35.4% (fastest-growing, 8.2% CAGR), Beryllium Oxide 12.3%, Silicon Nitride 8.8%.
- Application split: Automotive 35.7%, Consumer Electronics 28.4%, Industrial 19.2%, Telecommunications 11.8%.
- Kyocera Corporation holds ~18-20% of this broader market — the single largest player, with facilities in Japan, Southeast Asia, China, Germany, and the US.
- Other major players: Murata Manufacturing (Nagoya, Japan — high-frequency/RF substrates), CoorsTek (US), CeramTec (Germany), Maruwa (Japan — AlN/RF), Rogers Corporation (portfolio player, not primary focus).
- AlN vs Alumina in RF: AlN has 50-70% lower signal attenuation at 10 GHz but costs 2.5-3.5x more.
