## Summary
A QYResearch report (distributed via Taiwan's GII) forecasting the global AMB-only ceramic substrate market to grow from USD 443.46 million in 2024 to USD 1,307 million by 2030, at a 19.74% CAGR — a seventh distinct AMB-only market-size trajectory in this wiki, and notably a different QYResearch edition/figure than the one already ingested in [[source]] (QYResearch Korea briefing).

## Key Claims
- Global AMB ceramic substrate market: USD 443.46M (2024) → USD 1,307M (2030), 19.74% CAGR.
- Regional breakdown: North America USD 25.48M (2024) → USD 64.61M (2030), 16.77% CAGR; China USD 180M (2024) → USD 621.7M (2030), 22.94% CAGR; Europe USD 138.2M (2024) → USD 353.9M (2030), 16.97% CAGR.
- Key manufacturers: Rogers Corporation, Ferrotec, BYD, Toshiba Materials, Heraeus Electronics, Denka, Proterial, Mitsubishi Materials — top 8 held ~85% revenue share in 2023.
- Production concentrated in Japan (27.3%), Germany (24.56%), China (43.5%) as of 2023; China's production share projected to reach 57% by 2030. NGK Electronics Devices and Ferrotec have started/built AMB factories in Malaysia — Southeast Asia emerging as a production base.
- Si3N4 AMB substrates >95% of the segment; new-energy-vehicle application ~81.6% share in 2023, rising to 87.8% by 2030 (20.79% CAGR).
- Notable candor: "there are already signs of overcapacity. The current competition is very fierce, resulting in a significant decline in the price of AMB ceramic substrates this year... the AMB substrate market will have great potential in the future, but it is still full of risks."
