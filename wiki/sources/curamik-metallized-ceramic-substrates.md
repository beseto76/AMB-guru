## Key Claims
- curamik® substrates consist of pure copper bonded or brazed to a ceramic substrate, designed to carry higher currents, provide higher voltage isolation, and operate over a wide temperature range.
- Value proposition: high heat conductivity, high heat capacity, and thermal spreading via thick copper cladding; low coefficient of thermal expansion versus metal- or plastic-based substrate alternatives.
- Six curamik product lines: curamik® Endurance, curamik® Performance, curamik® Performance Plus, curamik® Power, curamik® Power Plus, curamik® Thermal.
- Target applications span power electronics broadly: EV/HEV converters, inverters and electric powertrains; eTrucks/eBusses/forklifts; wind/solar converters and solar inverters; smart grid transmission & distribution; VFD and UPS; commercial avionics; satellite power management; propulsion systems; air conditioning/heating; white goods; power transistors and semiconductor elements.
