## Summary
Ferrotec Holdings' own FY3/22 investor-relations presentation (May 2022) — a primary source giving the first company-specific financial and capacity detail on [[Ferrotec]] in this wiki, which previously appeared only in "named key player" rosters with no differentiating data.

## Key Claims
- FY3/22 results: net sales ¥133,821M (+46.6% YoY), operating income ¥22,600M (+134.4% YoY), net income ¥26,659M (+221.9% YoY) — all record highs. Operating margin rose from 10.6% (FY3/21) to 16.9% (FY3/22).
- Increased AMB production output for power substrates and established a research institute anticipating EV market growth.
- China factory network includes Dongtai No.2 (power semiconductors, DCB) and Dongtai No.3 (power semiconductors, AMB) — both reaching full operation (≥80% utilization) in the 2021-2023 window per the factory-status chart, alongside Changshan (quartz/metal processing) and Yinchuan (SiP/silicon parts).
- Updated mid-term targets: net sales ¥180,000M (FY3/23 forecast) → ¥230,000M (FY3/24 plan) → ¥290,000M (FY3/25 plan), up from a prior plan of ¥150,000M by FY3/24 — achieved a year ahead of schedule.
- Long-term vision (FY2030/FY3/31): net sales target raised to ¥500,000M (from a prior ¥300,000M goal), net income target ¥50,000M.
- Capital increases: ¥19.3B at the parent level; ¥8.3B specifically at the power-substrate subsidiary (Jiangsu Ferrotec Semiconductor Technology) to fund AMB/DCB capacity growth. Equity ratio raised to 49.5%.
- "Electronic device (EV, etc.)" segment — which includes power semiconductor substrates — grew from ¥27,024M (FY3/21) to a planned ¥51,164M (FY3/24), +89.3%.
- Discusses COVID-19 Shanghai lockdown supply-chain disruption (2022): raw-material delays via Port of Shanghai, temporary export suspension, factory staff sheltering on-site to maintain production.
