## Summary
A Dataintelo report sizing the combined DCB+AMB power-module substrate market at USD 1.8 billion in 2025, growing to USD 3.6 billion by 2034 at an 8.1% CAGR — a third, distinct combined-market figure alongside the Transparency Market Research ($2.1B→$4.7B) and QYResearch/GII ($996M→$2,148M) estimates already in the wiki. DCB holds 62.4% share (cost-effective, legacy-dominant); AMB is the faster-growing segment (10.2% CAGR) gaining ground in SiC automotive traction applications.

## Key Claims
- Combined DCB+AMB market: USD 1.8B (2025) → USD 3.6B (2034), 8.1% CAGR.
- DCB substrates: 62.4% share (~$1.12B), 6.8% CAGR — Al2O3/AlN ceramic base, cost-effective, dominant in industrial/legacy automotive.
- AMB substrates: 37.6% share, 10.2% CAGR (faster-growing) — Si3N4/AlN ceramic base via Ag-Cu-Ti braze; withstands 3,000+ thermal shock cycles (ΔT>100K) vs. 1,500-2,000 for DCB/AlN; commands 2-3x price premium over DCB/Al2O3.
- AMB/Si3N4 preferred for SiC power modules: thermal conductivity 70-90 W/m·K, CTE ~3.0 ppm/K (closely matches SiC's 3.7 ppm/K), fracture toughness ~6-7 MPa·m^0.5 vs. ~3.5 for Al2O3.
- Automotive application: 34.5% share, fastest-growing at 10.8% CAGR, driven by 800V architecture adoption (Porsche Taycan, Hyundai IONIQ 6).
- OEMs = 73.8% of end-user demand; aftermarket (repair/replacement in rail, UPS) = 26.2%.
- Top 5 suppliers control ~54% of global revenue. Rogers Corporation is the global leader (Curamik brand); FY2024 net revenue ~$843M. Rogers commissioned an expanded AMB line at Eschenbach, Germany (April 2025) to serve European SiC demand.
- Japan produces >60% of global high-purity Si3N4 ceramic powder — a concentrated supply-chain risk.
- Chinese entrants: Nanjing Zhongjiang New Material Technology, Shenzhen Danbond Technology — improving DCB quality, beginning to qualify AMB/Si3N4 for domestic SiC customers.
