## Summary
An official NGK Corporation press release (7 March 2024) announcing a decision to increase monthly AMB substrate production capacity by approximately 2.5x (from ~100,000 to ~250,000 units/month) by fiscal year 2026, investing approximately 5.0 billion yen, targeting 20.0 billion yen in AMB-related net sales by fiscal year 2030. This is a primary-source corroboration of the AMB capacity-expansion trend described in the market-research sources.

## Key Claims
- NGK INSULATORS, LTD. (Nagoya, Japan) decided to expand AMB substrate production capacity for power modules by ~2.5x by FY2026.
- Monthly production capacity increases from ~100,000 to ~250,000 AMB substrates by FY2026; investment ~5.0 billion yen.
- Target: 20.0 billion yen in net sales from AMB substrates by FY2030, driven by automotive-application demand growth.
- Corporate structure: NGK CERAMIC DEVICE CO., LTD. (NCDK, Komaki, Aichi) handles front-end processing (bonding); NGK ELECTRONICS DEVICES, INC. (Mine, Yamaguchi, Japan) and NGK ELECTRONICS DEVICES (M) SDN.BHD. (Penang, Malaysia) handle back-end processing (etching, plating). Investment will expand facilities at NCDK and the Malaysia subsidiary.
- NGK is also planning an additional AMB production base in Europe to strengthen supply chain to key power-module markets.
- NGK's Si3N4 AMB substrates use proprietary bonding technology enabling extremely thin (few-micron) bonding layers between ceramic and copper, reducing thermal resistance and internal strain.
- Adopted by several European and Japanese power module manufacturers since 2019; increasingly used with SiC power semiconductors requiring stability at high power/high temperature.
- Part of NGK Group's "New Value 1000" mid-to-long-term vision (targeting 2050, Carbon Neutral/Digital Society focus): at least 100.0 billion yen in net sales from new business fields by 2030, with AMB substrates contributing via BEV/HEV applications.

## Key Quotes
> "By the fiscal year 2026, monthly production will be increased by about 2.5 times from the current capacity. NGK aims for net sales of 20.0 billion yen by the fiscal year 2030."
