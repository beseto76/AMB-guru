## Summary
A Transparency Market Research (TMR) report covering the combined DCB and AMB substrate market (not AMB alone), valued at US$ 2.1 billion in 2024, projected to reach US$ 4.7 billion by 2035 at a 7.7% CAGR, driven by transportation electrification and renewable energy expansion. Asia-Pacific holds 67% revenue share. Notably, this report finds alumina-based (Al2O3) substrates dominate the combined market, unlike the AMB-only reports which found Si3N4 dominant.

## Key Claims
- Combined DCB + AMB substrate market: US$ 2.1B (2024) → US$ 4.7B (2035), CAGR 7.7% (2025–2035).
- Alumina-based (Al2O3) substrates dominate the overall DCB+AMB market due to balanced performance and lower cost, widely used in consumer electronics, industrial control, and automotive medium-power modules (e.g. a South Korean white-goods manufacturer reported a 15% thermal-efficiency gain switching from PCB to alumina-based DCB substrates).
- Asia-Pacific holds 67% of global revenue share, led by China, Japan, South Korea, and Taiwan; China notably strong in EV manufacturing, solar installation, and industrial automation.
- Rogers Corporation, Kyocera, and Ferrotec are highlighted as big players investing heavily in capacity expansion and quality improvement.
- More than 15 million EVs were sold worldwide in 2024; Tesla, BYD, and Volkswagen are named as integrating DCB and AMB substrates into their power modules.
- Key players list: Rogers Corporation, Ferrotec, BYD, NGK Electronics Devices, Heraeus Electronics, Kyocera Corporation, Mitsubishi Materials Corporation, Denka Company Limited, Proterial, DOWA METALTECH Co., Tong Hsing Electronic Industries, Maruwa Co., CeramTec GmbH, Remtec Inc., Stellar Industries Corp.
- Growth drivers: electrification of transportation, renewable energy expansion (solar/wind inverters and converters), rising use of wide-bandgap semiconductors (SiC, GaN).

## Contradictions
- **Scope mismatch with prior AMB-only sizing:** This source values the *combined* DCB+AMB substrate market at only US$ 2.1B in 2024, yet [[amb-ceramic-substrate-market-report-dynamics-growth-drivers-future-outlook-2025-2033]] (Congruence) values the *AMB-only* market at US$ 2,434.1M (~US$ 2.4B) in 2025 — i.e., a claimed AMB-only subset exceeding the claimed DCB+AMB combined total for a nearby year. This is very hard to reconcile and suggests at least one of the three market-sizing sources ingested so far (Intel Market Research, Congruence, TMR) is using an inconsistent market definition or methodology.
- **Material-type contradiction:** [[amb-ceramic-substrate-market-2026-to-2034-intel-market-research]] and [[amb-ceramic-substrate-market-report-dynamics-growth-drivers-future-outlook-2025-2033]] both report Si3N4 as the dominant/fastest-growing AMB substrate material (>95% and ~27%/fastest-growing respectively). This TMR source instead finds alumina (Al2O3) substrates dominate the (combined DCB+AMB) market. This is plausibly reconcilable — alumina dominance is likely driven by the DCB portion of the combined market rather than the AMB portion — but the source itself does not disaggregate DCB vs. AMB material share, so this should be treated as an open reconciliation point rather than a resolved one.
