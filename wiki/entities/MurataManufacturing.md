## Summary
A major Japanese manufacturer (Murata Manufacturing Co., Ltd., Nagoya, Japan) specializing in passive components and high-frequency substrate materials, named as a top-tier player in the broader ceramic substrate market alongside [[Kyocera]]. Not named in any of the AMB-only or DCB+AMB-specific market reports ingested so far — its substrate business appears weighted toward RF/telecom rather than power-module AMB/DBC.

## Details
- Maintains a significant market position through expertise in high-frequency substrate materials and integration with major telecommunications and automotive customers.
- Cited by name in the ceramic substrate market's competitive-landscape discussion as a peer of Kyocera, CoorsTek, and CeramTec.
