## Summary
A U.S.-based advanced materials manufacturer cited as one of the two leading technology innovators (alongside [[Ferrotec]]) in the global AMB ceramic substrate market.

## Details
- Named among the top 8 AMB ceramic substrate players that collectively held 85% market share in 2023.
- Profiled as a key manufacturer in Intel Market Research's AMB substrate market report.
- Since May 2023, has been expanding power substrate production capacity in China for its curamik® AMB and DBC substrate lines to meet EV, HEV, and renewable-energy demand; first phase completion expected 2025.
- Makes curamik® AMB Si3N4 substrates with >90 W/m·K thermal conductivity, described as the "IGBT preferred choice for electric vehicles" ([[top-10-factories-for-metallized-ceramic-components]]).
- Per Rogers' own product page, the curamik® brand spans six product lines (Endurance, Performance, Performance Plus, Power, Power Plus, Thermal) and covers both bonded (DBC) and brazed (AMB) copper-ceramic substrates — a broader technical scope than the AMB-only framing in most market-research sources ([[curamik-metallized-ceramic-substrates]]).
- Per Rogers' Active Metal Brazing technical page: curamik AMB uses a Si3N4 ceramic isolator (90 W/m·K thermal conductivity, CTE 2.6 ppm/K), with copper layers up to 800µm thick ([[active-metal-brazing-rogers-corporation]]).
- Global market leader in the combined DCB+AMB substrate market per Dataintelo (~top of a market where top-5 suppliers hold ~54% share); FY2024 net revenue ~$843M. In April 2025, commissioned an expanded AMB production line at Eschenbach, Germany to serve growing European SiC module demand ([[dcb-and-amb-substrates-for-power-modules-market-research-report-2034]]).
- Ranked #2 AMB-only supplier by revenue share (14.7%, behind Kyocera's 18.2%) per Dataintelo's AMB-specific report ([[active-metal-brazed-amb-substrates-market-research-report-2034]]).
