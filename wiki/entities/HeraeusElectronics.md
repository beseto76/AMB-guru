## Summary
A specialized substrate manufacturer, noted for maintaining a strong position in high-performance segments like automotive power modules where substrate reliability is critical (alongside [[NGKElectronicsDevices]]). Parent company is Heraeus Holding GmbH (Germany), a global leader in aerospace/medical vacuum sealing and high-precision Mo-Mn metallization.

## Details
- Major AMB substrate supplier with deep expertise in Si3N4-based products, serving European automotive and industrial OEMs ([[dcb-and-amb-substrates-for-power-modules-market-research-report-2034]]).
- Ranked #3 AMB-only supplier by revenue share (12.3%) per Dataintelo, with strength in automotive/industrial applications; integrated portfolio spans substrates, thick-film circuits, and hybrid integration services ([[active-metal-brazed-amb-substrates-market-research-report-2034]]).
- March 2026: officially announced an electrical life prediction model for Si3N4 ceramic substrates at CIPS 2026 (Germany), enabling quantitative evaluation of insulation reliability/service life to optimize power-module design and streamline certification testing ([[global-amb-ceramic-substrate-industry-report-2026-market-share-competitive-landscape-and-17-2-growth]]).
