## Summary
A materials/components manufacturer (Ferrotec Holdings Corporation, Japan) cited as one of the two leading technology innovators (alongside [[RogersCorporation]]) in the global AMB ceramic substrate market, and named as part of the "Top 3" AMB suppliers (with [[Denka]] and Rogers) in the Korean-language QYResearch briefing. Now has substantive company-specific financial and capacity data from its own investor-relations disclosures.

## Details
- Named among the top 8 AMB ceramic substrate players holding 85% collective market share in 2023 (Intel Market Research).
- Named as part of a "Top 3" group holding 80% share per QYResearch Korea's briefing — see [[source]].
- FY3/22 (fiscal year ended March 2022) results: net sales ¥133,821M (+46.6% YoY), operating income ¥22,600M (+134.4% YoY), net income ¥26,659M (+221.9% YoY), all record highs; increased AMB production output for power substrates and established a research institute anticipating EV market growth ([[ferrotec-holdings-corporation-update-of-the-mid-term-management-plan-ir-street]]).
- China factory network: Dongtai No.2 (power semiconductors, DCB) and Dongtai No.3 (power semiconductors, AMB), both at/near full operation as of FY3/22.
- Long-term vision (as of May 2022): net sales target raised to ¥500,000M by FY2030 (from a prior ¥300,000M goal).
- April 2025: completed and began full-scale production at a new Si3N4-AMB/DCB power-substrate factory in Malaysia, focused initially on automotive inverters ([[global-amb-ceramic-substrate-industry-report-2026-market-share-competitive-landscape-and-17-2-growth]]) — corroborating and advancing the 2022 mid-term plan.
