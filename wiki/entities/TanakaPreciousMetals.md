## Summary
A Japanese precious-metals manufacturer (TANAKA Precious Metal Group / TANAKA Precious Metal Technologies) supplying the Ag-Cu-Ti active brazing filler metals used to bond copper to ceramics in AMB substrate manufacturing — a materials supplier one level upstream of the AMB substrate makers ([[RogersCorporation]], [[Ferrotec]], etc.) profiled elsewhere in this wiki.

## Details
- Flagship active brazing filler metal: TKC-661 (AgCuTi alloy: 66% Ag, 29.5% Cu, 1.5% Ti). Solidus 745°C, liquidus 780°C, thermal conductivity 102.0 W/m·K, tensile strength 356 MPa.
- Also produces an "Active Brazing Filler Metal/Copper Composite Material" — braze pre-clad onto copper foil — for next-generation power-device heat sinks and ceramic substrates, reducing manufacturing process steps.
- July 2021: announced development of this active metal brazing material/copper composite specifically for power devices (per company news release cited on its product page).
- Positions itself in Consumer Electronics, Semiconductor, and Joining/Sealing (Packaging) markets.
