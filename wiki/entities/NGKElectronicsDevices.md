## Summary
A specialized AMB ceramic substrate manufacturer noted for a strong position in high-performance segments, particularly automotive power modules where substrate reliability is critical. Part of the NGK corporate group (parent: NGK Insulators, Ltd., Nagoya, Japan; also referred to as "Niterra" in its rebranded materials business).

## Details
- Also referred to as "Niterra Materials" (NGK's rebranded materials business) in some company-profile lists.
- Named alongside Heraeus Electronics as a specialized manufacturer maintaining strong positions in high-performance segments (Intel Market Research).
- Corporate structure per NGK's own March 2024 press release: NGK Insulators, Ltd. (parent, Nagoya) → NGK Ceramic Device Co., Ltd. (NCDK, Komaki, Aichi — front-end bonding) and NGK Electronics Devices, Inc. (Mine, Yamaguchi, Japan — back-end etching/plating), plus NGK Electronics Devices (M) Sdn. Bhd. (Penang, Malaysia — back-end).
- March 2024: decided to increase AMB substrate monthly production capacity ~2.5x (from ~100,000 to ~250,000 units) by FY2026, investing ~5.0 billion yen; targeting 20.0 billion yen in AMB net sales by FY2030.
- Planning an additional AMB production base in Europe.
- Si3N4 AMB substrates adopted by European/Japanese power module manufacturers since 2019; increasingly used with SiC power semiconductors.
- Also makes metallized ceramics for sensors; described as a "core supplier for automotive electronics" ([[top-10-factories-for-metallized-ceramic-components]], listed as "NGK/NTK").
- Leading supplier of AlN-based DCB substrates in the Japanese power module industry ([[dcb-and-amb-substrates-for-power-modules-market-research-report-2034]]).
- May 2025: exhibited SN-AMB/DCB substrates, SiC wafers, and high-current ceramic wiring substrates together at PCIM Europe 2025, emphasizing integrated system proposals (heat dissipation design + wiring pattern + wafer characteristics) rather than a component-only pitch ([[global-amb-ceramic-substrate-industry-report-2026-market-share-competitive-landscape-and-17-2-growth]]).
- That same 2026 QYResearch report lists "NGK Corporation" and "Niterra Materials" as two separate top-10 entries — likely reflecting NGK Insulators' 2023 global rebrand of parts of its materials business to Niterra, but the report doesn't clarify the distinction; flagged as an open naming question rather than resolved fact.
