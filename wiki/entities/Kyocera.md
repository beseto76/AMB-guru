## Summary
A Japanese ceramics and electronics manufacturer, named as a key player in the global AMB ceramic substrate market and a leading Asia-Pacific regional player expanding ceramic substrate production for automotive electrification.

## Details
- August 2024: broke ground on a new fine-ceramics production facility in Isahaya City, Nagasaki Prefecture, Japan, to strengthen capacity for semiconductor/power-electronics ceramic components; operations planned to begin 2026.
- September 2025: signed a joint development agreement with Kyoto Fusioneering Ltd. to co-create advanced ceramic materials for next-generation fusion energy applications.
- Investing in expanded ceramic substrate lines to meet automotive electrification demand in Asia-Pacific.
- Makes DBC/AMB ceramic substrates and HTCC packages; described as a "global power module leader supplying Tesla" directly ([[top-10-factories-for-metallized-ceramic-components]]).
- Market leader (~18-20% share) in the much broader ceramic substrate market (USD 8.7B in 2025, spanning alumina/AlN/BeO/Si3N4 across automotive, consumer electronics, and telecom), with facilities in Japan, Southeast Asia, China, Germany, and the US ([[ceramic-substrate-market-research-report-2033]]).
- Ranked #1 AMB-only supplier by revenue share (18.2%) per Dataintelo's AMB-specific report — ahead of Rogers (14.7%) and Heraeus (12.3%) ([[active-metal-brazed-amb-substrates-market-research-report-2034]]).
