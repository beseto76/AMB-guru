## Summary
A Chinese manufacturer of AMB/DBC ceramic-copper substrates, described as having the largest domestic AlN metallization capacity in China, supplying BYD and Huawei.

## Details
- Named as an emerging Asian player aggressively gaining share in the AMB market (alongside [[BYD]]) per [[amb-ceramic-substrate-market-2026-to-2034-intel-market-research]].
- Ranked #1 AMB supplier by revenue share in QYResearch's 2026 industry report — the only source in this wiki to rank a Chinese manufacturer above Rogers/Ferrotec/Kyocera/Denka, a notable divergence from the "Top 3 = Denka/Rogers/Ferrotec" narrative established elsewhere ([[global-amb-ceramic-substrate-industry-report-2026-market-share-competitive-landscape-and-17-2-growth]]).
