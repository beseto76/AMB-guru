## Summary
A South Korean semiconductor company (LX세미콘) highlighted by QYResearch Korea as a promising domestic AMB substrate player, building a new heat-dissipation substrate production plant using an adhesive-free diffusion bonding process to join ceramic and copper.

## Details
- New heat-dissipation substrate plant planned to begin operation within 2023.
- Uses diffusion bonding (no adhesive) between ceramic substrate and copper, aiming for higher thermal and mechanical reliability than adhesive-bonded alternatives.
