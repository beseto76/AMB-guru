## Summary
A South Korean materials company (아모그린텍) highlighted by QYResearch Korea as a promising domestic AMB substrate player, developing long-life, high-heat-dissipation AlN AMB substrates as a new growth driver alongside its core magnetic-materials business.

## Details
- Core magnetic-materials products are used in high-performance EVs from Tesla and Rivian.
- Building a new heat-dissipation substrate production facility, with operations planned within 2023.
- Developing AlN AMB substrates focused on long service life and high heat dissipation.
