## Summary
DuPont is an industry partner on NREL's Keystone Project 1, supplying Temprion polyimide film, the base material used to build organic direct-bond copper (ODBC) power module substrates as a ceramic-free alternative to AMB/DBC substrates.

## Details
- Susan Herczeg represents DuPont on the project team.
- Temprion polyimide shows high thermal stability (>500°C) and a wide processing window per thermogravimetric and dynamic mechanical analysis in the source.
