## Summary
A Japanese materials manufacturer named as a key player in both the AMB-only and combined DCB+AMB substrate markets.

## Details
- MetaCeram (AlN) and CuraMik (AMB/Si3N4) brands recognized for premium substrates used in high-performance automotive and industrial modules ([[dcb-and-amb-substrates-for-power-modules-market-research-report-2034]]).
- Technical collaboration with major automotive customers including Toyota, Honda, and Nissan on next-generation EV power module requirements.
- Ranked #5 AMB-only supplier by revenue share (8.6%) per Dataintelo, with integrated capabilities spanning substrate manufacturing, power module assembly, and IC packaging ([[active-metal-brazed-amb-substrates-market-research-report-2034]]).
