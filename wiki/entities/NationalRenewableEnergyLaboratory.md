## Summary
NREL is a U.S. national laboratory operated by Alliance for Sustainable Energy, LLC for the Department of Energy. It leads "Keystone Project 1," DOE Vehicle Technologies Office-funded R&D into ceramic-free organic direct-bond copper (ODBC) power module substrates as an alternative to traditional AMB/DBC ceramic substrates.

## Details
- Presented project status at the 2024 DOE VTO Annual Merit Review and Peer Evaluation Meeting (June 4, 2024).
- Principal Investigator: Douglas DeVoto. Task Leader: Sreekant Narumanchi.
- Collaborates with Oak Ridge National Laboratory (ORNL) and DuPont on this work.
- Funded under DOE Contract DE-AC36-08GO28308.
