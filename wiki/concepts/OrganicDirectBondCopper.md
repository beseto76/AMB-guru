## Summary
ODBC is an experimental, ceramic-free power module substrate developed by NREL/DuPont under DOE's Keystone Project 1, built on DuPont Temprion polyimide film instead of a ceramic base. It is being evaluated as a replacement for AMB and DBC ceramic substrates.

## Details
- Bonds at 300°C and 2.41 MPa pressure under vacuum or inert atmosphere — much lower than AMB's 850°C or DBC's ~1,065–1,083°C.
- No limitation on metal material or metallization thickness (unlike DBC's 1 mm cap).
- Maintained electrical and thermal performance after 5,000 thermal shock cycles in testing.
- In comparative thermal-resistance testing, an ODBC 1.0/0.025/1.0 mm sample showed the lowest thermal resistance (0.917) among the substrate samples tested, ahead of Si3N4 AMB, AlN, Al2O3, and HPS 9% samples.
- Goal: eliminate ceramic-related high-temperature and CTE-mismatch reliability concerns; targeted at electric vehicle power electronics.
- As of the June 2024 source, still in R&D — experimental characterization and full module assembly/reliability evaluation with industry were listed as remaining work.
