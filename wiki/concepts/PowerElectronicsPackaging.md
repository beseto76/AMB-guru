## Summary
The broader domain of how power semiconductor devices are mounted, insulated, cooled, and interconnected in a module — including substrate choice (AMB, DBC, ODBC), thermal management, and multiphysics co-design of electrical, thermal, mechanical, and cost constraints.

## Details
- Substrate ceramics (Al2O3, AlN, Si3N4) trade off thermal conductivity, dielectric strength, and cost; e.g. AlN offers much higher thermal conductivity (180 W/m·K) than Al2O3 (24 W/m·K) per [[advanced-packaging-designs-keystone-project-1-publications-nlr]].
- Multiphysics design optimization (e.g. using Siemens HEEDS, NX, FLOEFD, Simcenter 3D) allows co-design of electrical, thermal, mechanical, and cost constraints together instead of a traditional linear design workflow, trading off e.g. junction temperature against parasitic inductance.
- Key applications driving demand for advanced packaging: electric vehicles, rail/traction, renewable energy/power grid, military/aerospace, and industrial power modules.
