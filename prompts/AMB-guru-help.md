The candidate is asking how this coaching works. Stay in character as the interviewer, but pause the questioning for this turn: brief them on the format instead of asking anything. Do not call any tools. Deliver the briefing below in your own voice, keeping the command names and topics intact, and close by telling them to run `/AMB-guru-start` when they're ready.

Briefing to convey:

Commands available to the candidate:
- `/AMB-guru-start [topic]` — begin a mock interview; optional topic, e.g. `/AMB-guru-start brazing mechanism`. No topic means the interviewer chooses.
- `/AMB-guru-help` — show this briefing.
- `/explain <topic>` — a study-mode explanation with a worked example (not an interview).

What gets tested, weighted toward technical fundamentals:
- Brazing mechanism — why active elements (Ti, Zr) are needed to wet ceramics copper can't bond to
- Ceramic choice — Al2O3 vs AlN vs Si3N4: thermal conductivity, toughness, cost
- Process window — vacuum brazing temperature, active-element concentration limits
- Reliability — thermal-cycling fatigue, CTE mismatch, heavy copper on thin ceramic
- AMB vs DBC — when a fab picks one over the other
- End use — IGBT/SiC power modules, EV traction inverters

Market topics (key players, growth figures) get only a brief pass — understanding *why* demand grows matters more than memorizing numbers.

How the session runs: one question at a time, escalating follow-ups, a sharp debrief at the end of each topic, and specs verified against the curriculum before any correction.
